# Executive Summary

## Healthcare Analytics Intelligence

Healthcare Analytics Intelligence is an end-to-end healthcare analytics and business intelligence project that transforms **597,774 healthcare records** into actionable insights across patient, clinical, operational, financial, and hospital performance.

The solution integrates **86,400 patients, 120,000 admissions, 120,000 billing records, 271,341 diagnoses, and 33 hospitals** into a unified analytical environment.

## Business Focus

* Patient demographics and population intelligence
* Admission and readmission analysis
* Clinical outcomes and diagnosis patterns
* Hospital and ward performance
* Healthcare costs and funding
* Bed capacity and resource utilization

## Analytics Workflow

Raw Data → Excel & Python → Data Cleaning & Validation → SQL → MySQL & Snowflake → Power BI Data Modeling → DAX & Power Query → UI/UX Dashboard Design → Business Insights

## Dashboard Suite

The Power BI solution contains five analytical experiences:

1. **Healthcare Executive Overview**
2. **Patient Population Intelligence**
3. **Operations & Financial Intelligence**
4. **Clinical Patient Journey**
5. **Hospital Performance & Clinical Intelligence**

## UI/UX

The dashboards follow an executive-focused design approach with strong visual hierarchy, KPI prioritization, consistent layouts, interactive slicers, cross-filtering, drill-through analysis, and visual storytelling.

## Technology Stack

Excel, Python, Pandas, SQL, MySQL, Snowflake, Power BI, DAX, Power Query, ETL, Data Modeling, Data Visualization, UI/UX Design, KPI Design, Interactive Reporting, Cross-Filtering, Drill-Through Analysis, Business Intelligence

## Outcome

A complete healthcare analytics solution combining **data preparation, data warehousing, SQL analysis, business intelligence, interactive visualization, and premium UI/UX design** to support data-driven healthcare decision-making.
