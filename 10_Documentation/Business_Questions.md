# Business Questions

## Patient Population

- How many unique patients are served by the healthcare network?
- Which age groups represent the largest patient population?
- How are patients distributed across states?
- What proportion of patients are new versus returning?
- How does insurance coverage vary across the patient population?
- What proportion of patients receive BPL support?

## Admission & Utilization

- How many admissions are recorded?
- What proportion of admissions are emergency, elective, and OPD?
- Which wards handle the highest admission volume?
- How does admission volume change over time?
- Which hospitals handle the highest patient volumes?
- What is the average length of stay?

## Clinical Performance

- What are the most common diagnosis categories?
- Which diagnoses are associated with longer hospital stays?
- How does clinical complexity vary across age groups?
- Which patient segments have the highest readmission risk?
- What is the overall 30-Day Readmission Rate?
- What are the major patient discharge outcomes?

## Financial Performance

- What is the total healthcare expenditure?
- How much healthcare cost is covered through government subsidies?
- How much is paid directly by patients?
- Which billing categories contribute the most to healthcare costs?
- Which wards have the highest cost per admission?
- Which diagnosis categories generate higher healthcare costs?

## Hospital Performance

- Which hospitals have the highest patient volumes?
- How does patient volume compare with available bed capacity?
- Which hospitals have higher readmission rates?
- How does average length of stay vary across hospitals?
- How does hospital performance differ by hospital tier?

## Resource Planning

- How many beds are available across the healthcare network?
- Which hospitals have the highest bed capacity?
- How does patient demand compare with available capacity?
- Which wards experience the greatest utilization?
- Where could additional healthcare resources be required?

## Decision-Making

The analysis is designed to help healthcare stakeholders:

- Improve capacity planning
- Identify high-risk patient segments
- Reduce avoidable readmissions
- Monitor clinical outcomes
- Optimize hospital resources
- Control healthcare expenditure
- Improve operational performance
- Support data-driven healthcare decisions
