# KPI Definitions

## Patient KPIs

### Total Patients
Unique number of patients represented in the analytical dataset.

### Total Admissions
Unique number of hospital admissions.

### Patient MoM Growth
Month-over-month change in the number of patients.

### Average Previous Admissions
Average number of previous admissions per patient.

### BPL Support Rate
Percentage of patients with BPL support.

## Clinical KPIs

### 30-Day Readmission Rate
Percentage of admissions where the patient was readmitted within 30 days.

### Average Length of Stay
Average number of days patients remain admitted.

### Average Charlson Index
Average Charlson Comorbidity Index across admissions, representing overall patient complexity.

### Total Diagnoses
Total number of diagnosis records associated with admissions.

### Positive Discharge Rate
Percentage of admissions resulting in a recovered/positive discharge outcome.

## Operational KPIs

### ICU Admission Share
Percentage of admissions assigned to the ICU.

### Average Procedures per Admission
Average number of procedures performed per admission.

### Total Bed Capacity
Combined bed capacity across hospitals.

### Patient Volume per Bed
Patient volume relative to available hospital bed capacity.

## Financial KPIs

### Total Healthcare Cost
Total healthcare expenditure across billing records.

### Government Subsidy
Total amount covered through government healthcare subsidies.

### Patient Out-of-Pocket Cost
Total amount paid directly by patients.

### Average Cost per Patient
Average healthcare cost associated with each patient.

### Cost per Admission
Average healthcare cost associated with each admission.

## Dashboard Analysis

These KPIs are used throughout the Power BI dashboard suite to evaluate:

- Patient demand
- Clinical complexity
- Readmission risk
- Patient outcomes
- Hospital operations
- Resource utilization
- Healthcare expenditure
- Funding distribution

The KPIs are implemented using Power BI DAX measures and are designed to respond dynamically to dashboard filters and selections.
