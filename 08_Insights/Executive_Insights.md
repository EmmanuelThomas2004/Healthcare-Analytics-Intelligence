# Executive Insights

## Patient Demand

- The healthcare system manages 120,000 admissions across 86,400 patients.
- Emergency admissions represent approximately 55% of total admissions, making emergency care the primary demand driver.
- General Ward accounts for approximately 64% of admissions, indicating the highest utilization across inpatient wards.

## Patient Outcomes

- The 30-Day Readmission Rate is approximately 11.8%.
- Recovered patients represent the largest discharge outcome category.
- Older patient groups show higher readmission rates and greater clinical complexity.

## Financial Performance

- Total healthcare costs exceed ₹11.4 billion.
- Government subsidy and patient out-of-pocket payments contribute almost equally to total healthcare funding.
- Pharmacy and procedure-related costs represent major components of healthcare expenditure.

## Operational Performance

- The healthcare network includes 33 hospitals with a combined bed capacity of 14,535.
- Average Length of Stay is approximately 6.85 days.
- ICU admissions represent approximately 17% of total admissions, highlighting significant critical-care demand.

## Key Business Takeaways

1. Emergency care is the primary driver of patient volume.
2. General Ward has the highest admission utilization.
3. Readmission risk increases with patient age and clinical complexity.
4. Healthcare expenditure is substantial and requires continuous cost monitoring.
5. Bed capacity and critical-care utilization should be monitored to support resource planning.
